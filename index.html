How to play

  • Win a fight by taking the enemy king. There is no check: guard your own.
  • Each turn you get aether and draw a card (three to start a fight, five at most). Play as many as you can pay for, then move one piece.
  • Pieces move as in chess. Pawns step one square and become queens on the far row.
  • Before each fight arrange your army on the lit squares: click a piece, then where it should stand.
  • Your army is made of somebodies. Each has a temper that bends a rule. Hover a piece to see it.
  • The map shows each act. Choose where to go: fights, a merchant, a camp, or the unknown.
  • Crowns: lose a fight and you lose one. Lose all three and the run ends. A piece that falls in a fight you win sits out the next one.
  • Rocks block the way. Aether wells pay one more aether a turn to whoever of yours stands on them.
  • 1–5 play a card   Esc cancel   Enter start the fight   M mute. Drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom.
  • On a phone: tap to select and move. Press and hold a piece to read it. Tap a card to raise it, tap again to play it. Pinch to zoom.
